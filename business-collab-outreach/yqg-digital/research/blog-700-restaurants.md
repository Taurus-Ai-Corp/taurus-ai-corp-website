## Windsor Restaurants Yet to Go Digital 🤷🏼‍♂️

Ready for a shocking stat? A whopping 37% of Windsor-Essex restaurants **STILL DON’T HAVE A WEBSITE IN 2024**! That’s right—246 local restaurants are missing out on the digital game. In today’s world where [85% of diners check the menu online](https://www.touchbistro.com/blog/restaurant-industry-statistics/) before choosing where to eat, that’s like having an “invisible” restaurant!

![bicycle falling GIF](https://media1.giphy.com/media/v1.Y2lkPTc5MGI3NjExOHJwZm41NGh0dnBnYzEyZG92bzdiN3J4b2w5OW9hZTZsOGRpdnNhNSZlcD12MV9pbnRlcm5hbF9naWZfYnlfaWQmY3Q9Zw/KOS39Lrl3c4iA/giphy.gif)

And here’s the kicker: restaurants with a strong online presence see a serious boost in revenue. Even a half-star improvement in online ratings can [increase revenues by 5-9%](https://wisernotify.com/blog/online-review-stats/)!

So no, your Facebook page from 2018 doesn’t count as a website (you aren’t fooling anyone 🤡). With over a third of Windsor restaurants still offline, there’s a whole lot of money being left on the table!

## Methodology & Notes (The Really Exciti… pa..r….😴💤)

![Cat GIF by sheepfilms](https://media2.giphy.com/media/v1.Y2lkPTc5MGI3NjExaWRqcGJscHhndXBieW8xZ3lrMGoyMWpvMHhiZGFzc2Q4MWxrZDJldiZlcD12MV9pbnRlcm5hbF9naWZfYnlfaWQmY3Q9Zw/xT77XZrTKOxycjaYvK/giphy.gif)

Wondering how we got all this juicy data?

Here’s a peek behind the curtain (warning: it’s pretty nerdy! 🤓).

### How We Did It:

1. **Data Collection 🤖**

- Scraped Google Business Profiles across Windsor-Essex
- Searched every city, neighborhood, and “restaurants near me”
- Used AI and language models to help categorize everything

2. **Traffic Analysis 📊**

- Ran website URLs through ahrefs (fancy SEO tool)
- Analyzed traffic patterns and trends
- Combined data in good ol’ Google Sheets

3. **Quality Control ✨**

- Manual checks for accuracy
- Removal of giant chains and non-Windsor-Essex businesses
- Fixed restaurant categorization (fun fact: many restaurants just list themselves as “restaurant” instead of their actual cuisine type—not great for SEO, folks!)
- Cross-referenced data points

**Important Notes:**

- Traffic numbers typically underestimate actual visitors (especially for newer restaurants)
- Some restaurants might have slipped through our digital net (sorry we aren’t perfect 😮‍💨)
- **If we missed a restaurant and you think you might impact the rankings. Tell us – we’ll add it!**
- Data is best used for comparing restaurants rather than exact traffic numbers
- Website traffic doesn’t include social media or Google Business Profile clicks

Think of this like a restaurant ranking system—it’s not perfect, but it gives us a pretty good idea of who’s cooking up the most interest online!
