1. Methodology

This report was compiled using a three-step process, inspired by YQG Digital's excellent 700+ restaurant analysis:

  1. Data Collection: We scraped Google Business Profiles and HomeStars listings across all Windsor-Essex municipalities for home services categories (roofing, renovation, plumbing, HVAC, electrical, landscaping, windows/doors, masonry, general contracting).
  2. Digital Presence Audit: Each business was checked for: active website (Y/N), mobile responsiveness, Google Business Profile completeness, review count and rating, and basic SEO signals (title tags, meta descriptions, page speed).
  3. Traffic Estimation: Where websites existed, we used third-party traffic estimation tools to benchmark relative visibility.
Note on data accuracy: Third-party traffic estimates may undercount actual visitors, but they serve as excellent relative benchmarks for comparing local competitors against each other. National chains were excluded to focus on independent local businesses.
